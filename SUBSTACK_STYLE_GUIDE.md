# SUBSTACK_STYLE_GUIDE.md

Formatting conventions for Strategic Income Playbook installments on
Substack. Keep this updated as conventions change.

## Post titles

No "Chapter N:" prefix -- the book keeps its real internal chapter
numbers, but Substack posts are titled by content only. E.g. "The
Dividend Spectrum: From Blue Chips to Synthetics", not "Chapter 1:
The Dividend Spectrum."

## Closing every installment

End each post with two short sections, in this order:

**Recap:** 2-3 sentences summarizing the core takeaway of *this*
installment. Not a full re-summary -- just the one idea a reader
should walk away with.

**Next installment:** 2-3 sentences teasing the next piece's real
hook -- specific enough to earn a click, without giving away the
punchline. Name a concrete detail (a rule, a number, a mechanism)
rather than a vague "stay tuned."

### Template

    ---

    **Recap:** [core takeaway in 2-3 sentences]

    **Next installment:** [concrete hook for what's coming, 2-3
    sentences]

    ---

### Example (from the Reinvestment Mechanics installment)

    **Recap:** Reinvestment isn't a single setting you flip once and
    forget. DRIP is the right tool for a stable, appreciating anchor
    -- wrong tool for anything structurally prone to NAV decay. The
    Siphon Strategy is how you tell the difference in practice:
    extract the premium, strip the risk, harden it into real equity.

    **Next installment:** All of this theory means nothing without
    the tax structure to house it. Roth versus Traditional isn't just
    a preference -- one filing-status trap can silently zero out your
    Roth contribution room overnight, and the fix involves a rule
    most investors have never heard of until it costs them money.
    That's next.
